Homelessness and Shelter Access in Santa Clara County

An exploratory geographic analysis of homelessness and shelter resources across Santa Clara County, California.

Research Question

How does the geographic distribution of shelters compare with the distribution of people experiencing homelessness across cities in Santa Clara County?

2025 Homelessness and Shelter Map

Data Sources

2025 Santa Clara County Point-in-Time (PIT) Count and publicly available shelter location data.
