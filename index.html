What the Data Shows

San Jose has the largest count. Its 6,503 people represent 60.7% of the county total. Its unsheltered rate is 60.9%, compared with 69.8% countywide. Its 3,959 unsheltered people are still the largest absolute count among the cities shown.

Palo Alto has a high unsheltered share. Of 418 people counted, 399 (95.5%) were unsheltered and 19 were sheltered: a 21.00:1 ratio. Mountain View's ratio is 4.60:1, and Santa Clara's is 8.57:1. These figures describe where people were counted, but do not establish why people lacked shelter.

Percentages and counts answer different questions. Campbell reports 108 people, all unsheltered. San Jose has a lower unsheltered percentage but a much larger number of unsheltered people. Planning should consider both.

The shelter sample cannot establish adequate access. Four mapped sites are in San Jose and one is in Mountain View. A complete inventory, bed capacities, occupancy, eligibility, and travel times are needed to assess whether resources match need.

Sources and Methods

2025 Santa Clara County Point-in-Time report — countywide figures and jurisdiction table, page 18.

Shelter names, addresses, and coordinates come from the supplied project. Their current operation has not been independently verified.

The 13 mapped city totals sum to 10,389. The county total of 10,711 also includes unincorporated areas, confidential locations, and suppressed city counts. Confidential locations are not mapped. Morgan Hill's sheltered count remains suppressed.

  • Unsheltered % = unsheltered ÷ total × 100.
  • Sheltered % = sheltered ÷ total × 100.
  • U:S ratio = unsheltered ÷ sheltered.
  • County share = city total ÷ 10,711 × 100.

Percentages use one decimal place; ratios use two. Sheltered counts measure people, not bed capacity. PIT is a snapshot and does not establish causation.
