Analysis

San Jose has the largest reported count: 6,503 people, or 60.7% of the county total. Its 3,959 unsheltered people are the largest absolute unsheltered count among the cities shown.

Palo Alto has a high unsheltered share: 399 of 418 people, or 95.5%, were unsheltered. Its unsheltered-to-sheltered ratio is 21.00:1, compared with 4.60:1 in Mountain View.

Evaluate AI errors in both people and percentages. A large city can have a large numerical error but a smaller percentage error. Percentage errors help compare cities with different reported totals.

Look for repeated patterns. Consistent underestimation or overestimation across comparable predictions may indicate systematic error. A single comparison does not establish its cause. Record the AI model, prompt, run date, and whether it had access to the PIT report.
